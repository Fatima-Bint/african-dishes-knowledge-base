# Pilot Source Register

The seed data uses a deliberately small set of Ghana government cultural sources. The register records what each source is allowed to support in the demo; it is not a claim that one source alone settles every cultural question.

| Source | Publisher | Tier | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [From Farm, Forest, Coast and Savannah](https://ghanaculture.gov.gh/from-farm-forest-coast-and-savannah-the-cultural-foundations-of-ghanaian-cuisine/) | National Commission on Culture, Ghana | A — government/institutional | Selected names and documented community or regional associations for Ga Kenkey, Fante Kenkey, Kpokpoi, Akple, Tuo Zaafi, Tubaani and Akyeke |
| [Regional Representation and Diversity](https://ghanaculture.gov.gh/regional-representation-and-diversity/) | National Commission on Culture, Ghana | A — government/institutional | Pilot associations for Ampesi, Eto and Mpotompoto |
| [Culture — Food and Ceremonies](https://www.motac.gov.gh/culture/) | Ministry of Tourism, Culture & Creative Arts, Ghana | A — government | General public description used for the Fufu pilot record |
| [Traditional Foods: Akplijii, Aprapransa and Akpledze](https://www.motac.gov.gh/en/wp-content/uploads/Traditional_Foods_Akplijii_Aprapransa_and_Akpledze.pdf) | Ministry of Tourism, Culture & Creative Arts, Ghana | A — government | Community name forms and description used for the Akplijii pilot record |

## Review note

The demo marks these records as human-reviewed within the project workflow. “Reviewed” does not mean exhaustive community validation. The incubator phase should involve relevant cultural custodians and domain researchers before the dataset is represented as authoritative or complete.
